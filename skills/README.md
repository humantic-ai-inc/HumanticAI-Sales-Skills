# Humantic Sales Skills

Seven skills for sellers using Humantic AI through an AI assistant. A skill is a workflow the assistant runs for you: it knows what to look for, what to check, and what to hand back. You ask in your own words, for example "prep me for my meeting with Acme", and the matching skill takes over.

Every skill works without Humantic connected and gets sharper with it. Each one also uses your calendar, mail, CRM or meeting notes when your assistant can see them, and asks you for what would help when it cannot.

| Skill | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| [buyer-read](buyer-read/) | How one person communicates, what drives them, and how to approach them. Also fixes a thin, stale or wrong profile |
| [meeting-prep](meeting-prep/) | A one-page brief built around what the meeting has to produce and the people in the room |
| [pursuit-plan](pursuit-plan/) | A plan for winning a named account: why now, who has to move, the way in, and when to walk away |
| [prospecting](prospecting/) | A ranked list of who to work this week, with a reason to reach out attached to every name |
| [sales-email](sales-email/) | First pitches, rewrites, replies, follow-ups and multi-stakeholder emails, matched to the reader |
| [buying-committee](buying-committee/) | Maps the people who decide and works out how to move them as a group |
| [expansion-play](expansion-play/) | Finds what an existing customer should buy next, after checking the current rollout is working |
