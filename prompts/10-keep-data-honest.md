# 10 - Keep the data honest

Feedback prompts. All free, and they improve what everyone gets back.

[Back to the prompt index](README.md)

> These take seconds. Most people never use them, which is why bad matches persist instead of getting fixed.

---

## 52. Flag a wrong profile match

**When:** The profile is clearly a different person.

```text
This Humantic profile is the wrong person. The correct one is [correct
LinkedIn URL or email].
```

**What you get back:** Confirmation the correction was recorded.


---

## 53. Say whether a profile was useful

**When:** It was right, or it was not, and either is worth recording.

```text
Record feedback on the Humantic profile for [LinkedIn URL or email]: it
was [useful / not useful] because [one line].
```

**What you get back:** Confirmation. Usefulness feedback is what separates an accurate profile from an actionable one.


---

## 54. Report a profile that reads inaccurate

**When:** Right person, wrong read. Different problem from a wrong match.

```text
Record feedback on the Humantic profile for [LinkedIn URL or email]: the
personality read does not match how they actually behave. [One line on
what was off.]
```

**What you get back:** Confirmation.


---

## 55. Check what exists before spending

**When:** A habit worth building before any account work.

```text
List the Humantic account research we already have, so I can see what
exists before starting anything new.
```

**What you get back:** Everything already built under your account, most recent first.
