# 02 - Prepare for a meeting

One read across everyone in the room, rather than several separate lookups.

[Back to the prompt index](README.md)

---

## 7. Brief me on every attendee

**When:** More than one person is joining and you want a single briefing, not a stack of profiles.

**Better with:** Calendar, to skip listing the attendees yourself.

```text
I have a meeting with these people from [company]: [Name 1, LinkedIn URL
or email], [Name 2, LinkedIn URL or email], [Name 3, LinkedIn URL or
email]. Pull their Humantic profiles and give me one briefing: how each
of them communicates, what to lead with, and anything I should watch for
running the room with all of them in it.
```

**What you get back:** A combined briefing, including where attendees are likely to react differently to the same point.


---

## 8. Add account context to the attendee briefing

**When:** You want the company picture and the people picture in one answer.

```text
Before my meeting with [company], check whether we already have Humantic
research on them. If we do, give me the short summary alongside the
attendee briefing.
```

**What you get back:** Who is in the room and how to talk to them, plus what is known about the company, together.


---

## 9. Build a one-page pre-call brief

**When:** You want something you can read on the way in, or send to whoever is joining you.

```text
Build me a one-page pre-call brief for my meeting with [Name, LinkedIn
URL or email] at [company]. Cover who they are, how to approach them,
what to open with, and two questions worth asking. Keep it to one page.
```

**What you get back:** A structured brief rather than an open-ended profile dump.


---

## 10. Prepare for a renewal conversation

**When:** The relationship exists, the conversation is about keeping it.

**Better with:** CRM and support tickets, so open issues surface before the customer raises them.

```text
I have a renewal conversation with [Name, LinkedIn URL or email] at
[company]. Based on their Humantic profile, how should I frame the
renewal, and what would make this person hesitate?
```

**What you get back:** Framing matched to the person, plus the likely source of friction.


---

## 11. Prepare for a first discovery call

**When:** First real conversation, and you want to run it their way rather than yours.

```text
I have a first discovery call with [Name, LinkedIn URL or email]. Based
on their Humantic profile, how much detail do they want up front, how
direct should I be, and what kind of question will they answer properly?
```

**What you get back:** A read on pace, directness and question style for that person.
