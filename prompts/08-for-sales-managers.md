## 42. Read the room before you join a rep's call

**When:** You are joining a call you have not been part of.

```text
I'm joining one of my rep's calls with [Name, LinkedIn URL or email] at
[company]. Give me a quick read on who this person is, how they
communicate, and what I should let my rep lead on.
```

**What you get back:** Enough to be useful in the room without taking it over.


---

## 43. Coach a rep on a specific stakeholder

**When:** A rep is stuck with one person and you want to give them something concrete.

**Better with:** A meeting notetaker, so coaching is based on what the rep actually said rather than what they reported.

```text
One of my reps is struggling to get traction with [Name, LinkedIn URL or
email] at [company]. Based on their Humantic profile, what specifically
should the rep change about how they are approaching this person?
```

**What you get back:** Coaching points tied to that individual rather than general advice.

## 44. Review committee coverage on a deal

**When:** You want to know whether the deal is single-threaded.

```text
On the [company] deal we are engaged with [Name 1, role], [Name 2, role]
and [Name 3, role]. Based on their Humantic profiles and the account
research, who is missing from this picture, and where is the risk if our
main contact goes quiet?
```

**What you get back:** A coverage read and the single-point-of-failure risk.

## 45. Prepare a pipeline review with buyer context

**When:** You want the pipeline conversation grounded in the buyer, not just the forecast.

**Better with:** CRM, to test buyer-side movement against what the forecast claims.

```text
For these accounts: [company 1], [company 2], [company 3], pull the
recent buyer-intent signals and tell me which deals have genuine
movement on the buyer side and which are quiet.
```

**What you get back:** Buyer-side evidence against each account, to test what the pipeline says.


---

## 46. Brief the team on a target account

**When:** Several people are about to work the same account.

```text
We are opening [company name] as a team. Pull the Humantic research and
give me a briefing the whole team can use: what the company cares about,
how they buy, and who we should be trying to reach.
```

**What you get back:** A shared starting point so everyone works from the same picture.
